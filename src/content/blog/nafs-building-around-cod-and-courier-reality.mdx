In a cash-on-delivery business, “delivered” does not mean “finished.”

The parcel may have reached the customer, but the business still needs to know what was collected, what the courier owes, whether the payment matches the order and what happens when delivery fails.

That is why delivery and money need to stay connected throughout the order journey.

## Delivery and payment are related—but not identical

The warehouse and courier move the parcel. Finance follows the money.

An order can be delivered while its COD payment is still waiting to be received. A failed delivery can create a return, change available stock and remove the expected payment. Treating COD as a simple payment label hides all of that work.

NAFS keeps the delivery status and payment status visible beside the same order, while allowing each team to manage its own responsibility.

## One place should hide courier differences from the team

Courier providers use different status names, booking steps and ways of reporting failures. Your team should not need to learn a different operating process for every provider.

NAFS Courier is designed to give the business one consistent place for quotes, bookings and delivery updates. Pathao is the currently available provider. Other providers will only be shown as available after their complete booking and update journeys are ready.

When a provider response is unclear, NAFS can send the case for review instead of creating another parcel automatically.

## Local fit is about the workflow

Supporting Bangladesh commerce means more than showing BDT and familiar courier logos.

The real work includes phone-first customer details, local delivery areas, cash collection, settlement matching, failed deliveries, warehouse coordination and returns.

When those steps are connected clearly, the business can grow without adding another spreadsheet or chat thread every time order volume increases.
