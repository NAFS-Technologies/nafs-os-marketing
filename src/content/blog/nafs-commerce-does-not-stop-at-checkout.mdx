For the customer, checkout feels like the end of a shopping journey. For the business, it is where the work begins.

A confirmed order changes several things at once. Stock must be set aside. A warehouse or store must take responsibility for fulfillment. Payment needs to be followed. A parcel may need a courier booking. If delivery fails or a customer requests an exchange, stock and money change again.

The challenge is not having a screen for each task. The challenge is keeping every task connected to the same sale.

## One order should carry the full story

When teams work from separate tabs, spreadsheets and chat messages, the order loses context as it moves between people.

Operations may know the customer and payment. The warehouse may see only the items. The courier dashboard may show only a parcel reference. Finance may receive a settlement sheet days later. Every team has part of the story, but nobody sees the whole journey.

NAFS keeps the customer, products, price, payment, stock and delivery details connected as responsibility moves from one team to another.

## Every update should make the next step clearer

When an order is confirmed, stock can be reserved. When stock is ready, the warehouse can receive a picking task. When packing is complete, delivery can begin. When a parcel is delivered, COD or payment matching may still remain open.

The important result is simple: people should not need to remember the next step or rebuild the context before acting.

Automation can help with the repetitive parts. NAFS Flow can notify a team, request approval, check inventory or start follow-up work when an important event happens.

## Different people need different views

A customer storefront, warehouse queue, mobile scanner and finance view should not look the same. Each person needs a screen built for the job in front of them.

What should remain consistent is the information underneath: one sale, one connected history and a clear next action.

That is the idea behind NAFS. Checkout creates the order. The platform stays responsible for what happens next.
