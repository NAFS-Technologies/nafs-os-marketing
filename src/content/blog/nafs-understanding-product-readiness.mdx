A product family can contain implemented workflows, separately released applications and products still in development. Those are different commitments. The website should make the distinction visible.

## Guided rollout

This label means an implementation exists, but your business still needs configuration and acceptance. Relevant checks may include imported data, permissions, provider credentials, pickup locations and the exact workflow your team will use.

It is not a claim that every business can connect immediately or that every possible integration is ready.

## In development

These pages describe a product direction. They are not a promise that the product is generally available or included in an existing agreement. Delivery scope, readiness and commercial terms must be confirmed separately.

## Connection readiness

Authorization is only one part of a useful connection. A business mapping must also identify the right records, interpret states and quantities correctly, and handle failures without hiding uncertain outcomes.

Access to an external API catalogue is not the same as having a verified business integration for every tool in it.

## Demonstrations and real operation

The interactive previews on this site use synthetic records. They explain operating concepts; they do not perform actions in your business or demonstrate that a production deployment has passed acceptance.
